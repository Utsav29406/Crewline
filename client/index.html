Crewline HRMS

People · Time · Pay. Crewline is a web HR and attendance system for small companies. Staff sign in on a phone or computer; admins and HR manage everything from the same app.

Features

  • Attendance: check in and out with a face match, only inside the office geofence; late marks with a grace period, half days, and hours worked.
  • Breaks and lunch, tracked against the working day.
  • Leave requests and approvals, with leave balances.
  • Payroll: monthly salary runs and downloadable payslips.
  • Live map of who is checked in and where.
  • Reports, notifications, employees, geofences and company settings for admins and HR.
  • Optional Windows desktop tracker: periodic screenshots and activity while checked in, with automatic breaks when idle.
